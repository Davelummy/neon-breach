How to play

30-second briefing. Then deploy on Recruit if you’re new.

Move, look, fire.
Left thumb moves; drag the right side to look. FIRE shoots, RLD reloads, JET jumps.
MORE opens weapon, aim, sprint, melee and screen controls. Enter appears beside a vehicle. Live radio is vehicle-only; it needs an approved station. Rotating your phone does not restart the mission.
WASDMove. Q sprint. Mouse looks (click the view first).
LMB / R2Fire. Shift also fires (not sprint). RMB / L2 aim down sights.
Space / ✕Jet jump. Follow the objective marker to advance.
R / □Reload. Empty mags auto-reload.
1–6Switch unlocked weapons. Walk over medkits and ammo to collect; gun caches equip the gun shown.
F / R3Finisher — close range, high score.
E / △Enter / exit vehicles near extract.
EscPause. Options on DualSense.
PadMenus: stick + Cross confirm, Circle back.

Goal: clear each phase, reach the objective marker, extract alive. Progress saves on this device. Scores can appear on the world leaderboard or your party board when the server is available.
